How much do surf lessons cost in Barbados?

Here's what the island charges, what you get for it, and what we charge.

The going rate

Two-hour group lessons on the island run from about US$50 to US$80 per person. Private lessons are roughly double. Multi-lesson packages bring the per-lesson price down.

What's included

Board, leash and rash vest, always. Some schools include transport from certain areas, some include photos. Ask.

What we charge

US$75 per person for up to two hours in the water, maximum five people per instructor, board included, and you keep the board until 3:30pm to carry on practising. Cash on the day, no deposit. Private and group prices on request.

Why not just the cheapest

Group size. A lesson with ten people and one instructor is a board hire with a chat at the start. Five people max means you're pushed into waves and corrected every time. That's the difference between standing up and watching.

How to pay

Cash on the day in US or Barbados dollars. We don't take deposits, so if the surf is wrong we simply move you to another day.
