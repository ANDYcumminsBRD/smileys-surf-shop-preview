Is Barbados good for beginner surfers?

Yes. One of the best places in the world to learn, in fact. Here's why, plus the two things to watch for.

Warm water, all year

26 to 29°C. No wetsuit, no cold shock, no excuse. You can stay in for two hours and only come out because you're hungry.

Gentle waves with no crowds to speak of

Freights Bay, Drill Hall and Pebbles Beach give slow, forgiving waves over sand, with room for everyone. Compare that with a cold, crowded beach break at home.

Lessons are good value

Small groups, qualified instructors and two hours in the water for US$75. Many European surf towns charge the same for one hour in a group of ten.

Watch out for: the sun

It's strong even in the water. Rash vest, sun cream before you arrive, water to drink.

Watch out for: the east coast

Beautiful and powerful. Beginners should look, not paddle out.
