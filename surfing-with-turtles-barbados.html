Surfing with turtles in Barbados

Almost every review of our lessons mentions the turtles. Here's why, and how to be a good guest in their water.

Where they are

Freights Bay has resident green and hawksbill turtles that sit in the line-up and surface between surfers. Carlisle Bay, by the shop, is a marine park full of them, best seen from a kayak or paddleboard.

What they do

Ignore you, mostly. They come up for air, have a look, and sink back down. Children lose their minds. So do adults.

How to behave

Don't chase, don't touch, don't feed. Give them room to surface. If one's under your board, wait. They've been here longer than the surf schools.

See them without surfing

Our kayak tour crosses Carlisle Bay's wrecks and turtle grounds in flat, clear water.
