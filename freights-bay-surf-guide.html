Freights Bay: Barbados' best beginner wave

If you learn to surf in Barbados, there's a good chance it's here. Here's what you're looking at.

The wave

A long, slow, peeling left over sand and flat rock. It breaks a long way out and rolls for ages, which is exactly what you want when you're learning to stand. Waist to chest deep in the inside.

When

Most consistent November to April, rarely flat the rest of the year. Best in the morning before the wind. Mid to high tide is friendliest.

The turtles

Green and hawksbill turtles live in the bay and often surface in the line-up. Most lessons see one. You might get a fright. They won't.

Getting there

On the south coast near Oistins, twenty minutes from Bridgetown. If you've no transport, meet us at the shop and we'll sort it.

Crowds

It's the island's most popular learning wave, so there are other schools here too. Small groups and an instructor in the water are what keep it safe and fun. That's how we run it.
