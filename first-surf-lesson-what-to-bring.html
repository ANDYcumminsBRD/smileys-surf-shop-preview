Your first surf lesson: what to wear, bring and expect

You don't need anything you haven't already packed for a beach holiday. Here's the list, and what the two hours actually look like.

Wear

Swimwear you can move in. Board shorts or a one-piece beat a loose bikini for the pop-up. We give you a rash vest, which saves your chest from the board and your back from the sun.

Bring

A towel, water, sun cream (put it on before you arrive, not on the sand), and cash. Leave jewellery, watches and phones in the shop or your bag on the beach.

Fitness

If you can swim and climb a flight of stairs, you can surf. It's technique, not strength. Older beginners often do best because they listen.

The two hours

Fifteen minutes on the sand: safety, how a wave works, how to pop up. Then in the water, waist-deep, with the instructor pushing you into waves and correcting you between them. Most people stand up in the first hour. Then it's practice, and the board is yours until 3:30pm.

The thing everybody forgets

Water. Two hours in the Barbados sun, even in the sea, dries you out. Bring a bottle.
