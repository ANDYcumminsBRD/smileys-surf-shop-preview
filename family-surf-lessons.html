Family surf lessons in Barbados

Mum, Dad and the kids on the same wave. The lesson everyone talks about on the flight home.

At a glance
US$75per person, everyone
  • Children aged 10 and over, with a parent
  • One group, one instructor, max 5 of you
  • Big stable boards for everyone
  • Waist-deep water, gentle wave
  • Keep the boards till 3:30pm
  • Cash on the day
💬 Book a family lesson

Why ten and over

We teach children from ten up, with a parent in the group. At ten they're strong enough to paddle, listen and get themselves back to the beach, and that's what keeps it safe and fun. Younger brothers and sisters are welcome on the sand with the other parent.

What a family lesson looks like

Everybody does the same beach briefing, then everyone's in. Kids usually stand up first, which the parents never hear the end of. Trigger moves between you, pushing each person into their waves. Two hours flies.

Make a morning of it

Pebbles Beach and Drill Hall are gentle, shaded at the edges, with turtles in the bay. Bring a picnic. The boards are yours until half past three, so the kids can carry on while you sit down.

Questions

Things people ask

What age can children start?

Ten and over, with a parent in the lesson.

Do parents have to surf too?

No, but most do. One parent can watch from the beach with the little ones.

Is it safe?

Trigger is an ISA Level 2 instructor and a qualified lifeguard, the water is waist-deep, and he is in it with you.
