Hire a board

Which board

Learning or rusty? A big soft-top: it floats, it forgives, it catches everything. Surfed for years? Fish, longboards and performance boards, and if you tell us your height, weight and what you ride at home we'll put you on the right one.

Where to take it

Drill Hall and Pebbles Beach are directly below the shop. Freights Bay is twenty minutes along the south coast. Ask and we'll tell you what the tide and swell are doing that day, and where not to go.

Ding repair

Knocked it? Bring it in. We fix what we can in the shop and tell you honestly when it's a bigger job.

Questions

Things people ask

Do I need to book a board?

In the season, yes, message us the day before. Off-season, just come in.

Can you deliver a board to my hotel?

Ask us. If you're on the south or west coast we can often sort it.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No deposit. Cash on the day. We trust you with the board and the sea.
