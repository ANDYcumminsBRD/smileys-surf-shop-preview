Where to paddle

Carlisle Bay is one of the calmest stretches of water on the island: sheltered, shallow and clear enough to watch turtles and rays from your board. Pebbles Beach and the Boatyard are an easy paddle from Drill Hall.

First time on a SUP?

You'll be standing in five minutes. We show you how to stand, turn and get back on, and point you at the quiet water.

Things people ask

Do you do SUP lessons?

Yes, ask us. Most people only need the ten-minute talk.

Can children paddle?

On a double kayak with an adult, yes. Alone on a SUP from ten with a parent on the water.
