Surf guide

When is the best time to surf in Barbados?

Short answer: any month, for a beginner. Longer answer below.

The season: November to April

Winter swells from the North Atlantic light up the whole island. Freights Bay is at its most consistent, Soup Bowl does its thing, and the water is still 27°C. This is when the island is busiest too, so book lessons and boards a day or two ahead.

Summer: May to October

Smaller, warmer, quieter. Lovely for a first lesson: gentle waves, empty line-ups, lower prices on everything from flights to rum. September is the quietest month of the year on the island. Hurricane season technically runs June to November, but Barbados sits well south of most storm tracks.

Water temperature

26 to 29°C all year. No wetsuits, ever. A rash vest for the sun is all you need.

Time of day

Mornings. The trade winds pick up most afternoons and chop the surface. First lesson of the day is usually the cleanest.

Our honest advice

If you're a beginner, come whenever you're coming. If you already surf and want size, November to March.
