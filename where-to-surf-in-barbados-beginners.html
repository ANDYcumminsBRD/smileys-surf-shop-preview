Where to surf in Barbados as a beginner

Barbados has waves on every coast, but only a handful are right for your first week. Here's where we take people, and why.

Freights Bay, south coast

The beginner's wave. A long, slow left over sand and flat rock, waist-deep, with turtles sitting in the line-up most days. It works best from November to April, but it's rarely flat. Twenty minutes from Bridgetown, near Oistins.

Drill Hall (Brandons) and Pebbles Beach, the Garrison

Our home breaks, directly below the shop and about ten minutes from Bridgetown and the cruise port. Small, forgiving waves over sand, sheltered, with shade at the edges. Good for a first lesson and for families, and we are the surf school based here.

Surfer's Point and South Point

A step up. Good once you can stand and turn, with a bit more push and some rock underfoot. Come with a guide the first time.

Where NOT to learn

Soup Bowl at Bathsheba is a world-class wave over sharp reef with strong currents. Go and watch it. Don't learn on it. The same goes for most of the east coast.

Which one on the day?

That's our job. We pick the break that suits the swell, the wind, the tide and your group. Book a lesson and we'll tell you where to meet.
