Surf lessons

Most people are standing up within the hour.

We keep it to five people per instructor, pick the gentlest wave on the island for that day, and stay in the water with you. You keep the board until 3:30pm to carry on practising. Beginners, nervous swimmers, families, couples, groups: this is what we do every day.

  • US$75 per person · up to 2 hours in the water · max 5 per instructor
  • Boards, leashes and rash vests included
  • Ages 10 and over
  • Surf not right? We move you to another day, no charge

Why the Garrison

The closest surf to Bridgetown, the port and the south-coast hotels.

In port for the day?

Pebbles Beach is a ten-minute taxi from the cruise terminal. A two-hour lesson and you're back on board with time to spare. Cruise-day lessons

Families, couples, groups

Parents and kids (10+) learn together on the same wave. Schools, work team days and hen or stag groups: message us for a group price. Family lessons · Groups

More than surfing

Board and kayak hire, SUPs, and kayak trips over the Carlisle Bay shipwrecks, full of fish and turtles. Hire & tours

The shop

A proper surf shop, run by surfers.

Boards for every level, bodyboards, reef shoes, wax and leashes, and one of the best selections of locally made bikinis and rash vests on the island. Brigade House at the Garrison, right above Drill Hall and Pebbles Beach. Monday to Saturday, 10 to 4.

The story

Named after Smiley. Run by his family.

Peter “Smiley” Boland was one of the Caribbean's first surfers. Today the shop is run by the second and third generation: Trigger, an ISA Level 2 instructor and qualified lifeguard, Jodie, and young Koa, already catching waves. Lessons the way a surfing family would teach their own.
