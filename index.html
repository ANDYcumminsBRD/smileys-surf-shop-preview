Surf lessons

Two hours. Five people. One instructor in the water with you.

We pick the gentlest wave on the island for that day, usually Freights Bay or our home beaches at the Garrison, and stay in the water beside you. You keep the board until 3:30pm to carry on practising.

Who we are

Named after Smiley. Run by his family.

Peter “Smiley” Boland was one of the Caribbean's first surfers. The shop is run by the next two generations: Trigger, an ISA Level 2 instructor and qualified lifeguard, Jodie, and young Koa, already catching waves. Lessons the way a surfing family would teach their own.

Where, when and how to book

The shop: Brigade House, The Garrison, St Michael, Barbados. Monday to Saturday, 10am to 4pm. Closed Sunday. About ten minutes from Bridgetown and the cruise terminal.

Lessons: we meet at the beach that's working that day and confirm it when you book. No transport? Meet us at the shop.

Booking: WhatsApp Trigger with the day and how many of you. Cash on the day, no deposit.
