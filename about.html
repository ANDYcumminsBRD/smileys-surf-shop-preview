About

Three generations of Barbados surfing

The shop is named after Peter “Smiley” Boland, one of the Caribbean's first surfers. His family still run it.

Trigger & Jodie

Trigger (Robert Boland) is an ISA Level 2 surf instructor and a qualified lifeguard who has surfed Barbados his whole life, from Freights Bay to Soup Bowl. Jodie surfs, runs the shop with him, and is usually the one taking the photos. Their boy Koa is the third generation, already up and riding.

What people say about Trigger's teaching comes up in almost every review: he actually teaches. He tells you what to look for, shows you, and stays in the water beside you until you've got it.

Small by choice

Five people per instructor, never more. You get told what you're doing right and wrong straight away, which is how you learn.

Honest about ability

Tell us honestly how you swim and surf and we'll set the lesson up so everyone is safe and everyone has fun.

Local, by design

We stock Bajan-made bikinis and rash vests, send you to the right beach for the day, and tell you how to get around without a taxi.

Stay nearby

A studio five minutes from the waves.

We have a quiet studio apartment in Navy Gardens, close to Drill Hall, Pebbles Beach and the boardwalk. Book it on Airbnb or ask us. Prefer to stay on the water? Our friends at Zed's on the south coast have rooms with Surfer's Point on the doorstep; tell them Smiley's sent you.
