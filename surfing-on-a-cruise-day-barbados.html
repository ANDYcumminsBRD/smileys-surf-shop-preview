Surfing on a cruise-ship day in Barbados

Most cruise passengers never find out that the closest surf to the port is ten minutes away. It is. Here's how to do it.

The timings

Ships usually dock around 8am and leave late afternoon. A 9:30 or 10am lesson has you standing on a wave by eleven and back at the terminal well before one. Message us your ship and the date and we'll set the time.

Getting there

Taxis queue outside the terminal. Say 'Pebbles Beach, by the Garrison'. Ten minutes, a few dollars each. We meet you on the sand with the boards.

What to bring

Swimwear under your clothes, a towel, sun cream, water and cash (US dollars are fine). We provide the board, leash and a rash vest, and a hose-down afterwards.

Why not the south-coast schools?

They're excellent, but they're around Oistins: forty minutes each way on a cruise-day road. We're the only surf school at the Garrison. More surfing, less taxi, no watching the clock.

Afterwards

You're five minutes from the Garrison Savannah, Carlisle Bay and the boardwalk cafés. Back aboard with time to spare.
