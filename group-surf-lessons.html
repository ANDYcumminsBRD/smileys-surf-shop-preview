Schools and youth groups

Surfing teaches patience, balance, water safety and a healthy respect for the sea, and the kids don't notice they're learning any of it. We run school groups from age ten with teachers on the beach and ISA-qualified, lifeguard-trained instructors in the water.

Team-building that people actually enjoy

Nobody remembers the trust-fall afternoon. Everybody remembers the morning the finance team stood up on a wave. Two hours, a group photo, a cold drink after. Message us with your numbers and the date.

Hens, stags and birthdays

A surf lesson is the sober morning that makes the rest of the weekend. Mixed abilities are fine, we split the group so nobody is held back or left behind.

Questions

Things people ask

How big a group can you take?

Message us with your numbers. We bring in extra instructors for bigger groups.

Do you do a group price?

Yes. Tell us how many and when.

What about non-swimmers?

Tell us in advance. We keep them in waist-deep water and often they have the best morning of all.
