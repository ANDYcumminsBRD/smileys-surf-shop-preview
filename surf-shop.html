The surf shop

Boards, beachwear and local advice, chosen by people who surf every day. Brigade House at the Garrison, above Drill Hall and Pebbles Beach. Monday to Saturday, 10 to 4.

Boards

Soft-tops for learners, fish, longboards and performance boards, from brands surfers actually ride. Come in, tell us how you surf, and we'll put you on the right board. Buying a board is a conversation, not a click.

Local, by design

We stock Bajan-made bikinis, rash vests and bags wherever we can. Less shipping, more island, and you take home something nobody else on the plane has got.

Find us

Brigade House at the Garrison, a UNESCO World Heritage site, about ten minutes from Bridgetown and the cruise terminal, on the Hastings to Bridgetown bus route. Door shut? Call, we're probably on the beach.

Things people ask

Do you sell second-hand boards?

Sometimes. Ask us what's in.

Can I buy online?

Message us what you want and we'll sort it. Boards are best bought in person.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10am to 4pm. Closed Sunday.
