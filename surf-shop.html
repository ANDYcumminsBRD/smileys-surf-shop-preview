Shop

Smiley's Surf Shop, Brigade House, The Garrison

Everything you need for surfing, chosen by people who surf every day. Right above Drill Hall and Pebbles Beach. Monday to Saturday, 10 to 4.

At a glance
  • Surfboards for every level, from BDS $1,600
  • Bodyboards from BDS $110, reef shoes for adults and kids
  • Wax, leashes, fins and the bits you forgot
  • Locally made bikinis, rash vests and beachwear
  • Smiley's t-shirts and caps
  • Advice that's actually about surfing
Message the shop

Boards

Soft-tops for learners, fish, longboards and performance boards, from brands surfers actually ride. Come in, tell us how you surf, and we'll put you on the right board. Buying a board is a conversation, not a click.

Local, by design

We stock Bajan-made bikinis, rash vests and bags wherever we can. Less shipping, more island, and you take home something nobody else on the plane has got.

Find us

Brigade House at the Garrison, a UNESCO World Heritage site, five minutes from Bridgetown and the cruise terminal, on the Hastings to Bridgetown bus route. Door shut? Call, we're probably on the beach.
