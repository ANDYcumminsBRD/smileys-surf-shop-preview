Soup Bowl: Barbados' world-famous wave

Kelly Slater has called it one of his favourite waves on the planet. It's forty minutes from the shop. Here's the honest guide.

What it is

A powerful, hollow right-hander breaking over shallow coral reef off Bathsheba on the east coast. It's consistent, it barrels, and it's hosted world-tour events.

When it works

Best from November to May with the winter swells. The smallest waves are in July and August, when it's still head-high.

Who should surf it

Experienced surfers who are comfortable on reef in powerful waves. The take-off is shallow, the currents are strong and the reef is sharp. If you have to ask, watch from the beach this time.

Watching it

Bathsheba is beautiful: huge boulders, rock pools, the Round House for lunch. Go in the morning, sit on the wall and watch. It's one of the best free shows on the island.

Surfing it with a guide

If you're good enough and want someone who knows the line-up, the etiquette and the way out, ask Trigger about a guided session.
