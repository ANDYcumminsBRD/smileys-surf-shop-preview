Pebbles Beach and Drill Hall: surfing at the Garrison

The closest surf to Bridgetown, and our home. Most visitors walk past it. Here's what they're missing.

The waves

Small, sheltered beach breaks over sand on the edge of Carlisle Bay. Gentle enough for first lessons and children, with the odd fun day in winter. Drill Hall (locals say Brandons) picks up a bit more than Pebbles.

The setting

Casuarina trees for shade, calm water either side for swimming and paddleboarding, turtles in the bay, the Garrison Savannah behind. The racehorses come down to swim here in the mornings.

Getting there

Ten minutes by taxi from Bridgetown and the cruise terminal, on the Hastings bus route. Our shop is at Brigade House, directly above the beach.

Lessons and hire

We're the only surf school at the Garrison. Lessons, board hire, SUPs and kayaks all start from the shop and finish on this sand.
