Surf lesson on your cruise-ship day

Off the ship, into the water, back aboard with time to spare. Pebbles Beach is about ten minutes from the terminal, and we set the time around your ship.

US$75per person
  • Ten-minute taxi from Bridgetown cruise port
  • Two hours in the water
  • Boards, rash vests, hose-down included
  • Timed around your ship's hours
  • Ages 10 and over
  • Cash on the day, US dollars fine

How it works

Message us your ship and the date you dock. We agree a start time and the meeting beach with you, allowing plenty of margin for your all-aboard time. Taxis wait outside the terminal; tell them Pebbles Beach by the Garrison, or wherever we've agreed. We meet you on the sand with the boards.

Why the Garrison and not the south coast

The other surf schools are around Oistins on the south coast, a longer taxi each way on a cruise-day road. We are the surf school based at the Garrison, the closest surf to Bridgetown. More time surfing, less time in a taxi. If the surf is better at Freights Bay that day we'll tell you before you decide.

After your lesson

Rinse off at the shop, grab a locally made rash vest or bikini to take home, and you're a short walk from the Garrison Savannah, Carlisle Bay and the boardwalk cafés.

Things people ask

What if the ship is late or the surf is flat?

Message us and we move you. No deposit means nothing to lose.

Can we book through the ship?

Not yet. Book direct with us on WhatsApp, it's cheaper and quicker.

Is there somewhere to leave our bags?

At the shop, five minutes' walk, or on the beach with the group.
