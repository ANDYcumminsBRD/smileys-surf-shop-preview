Surf lesson on your Barbados cruise-ship day

Off the ship, standing on a wave by eleven, back on board for lunch. Pebbles Beach is ten minutes from the terminal.

At a glance
US$75per person
  • Ten-minute taxi from Bridgetown cruise port
  • Two hours in the water
  • Boards, rash vests, hose-down included
  • Timed around your ship's hours
  • Ages 10 and over
  • Cash on the day, US dollars fine
💬 Book a cruise-day lesson

How it works

Message us your ship and the date you dock. We fix a start time that gets you back aboard with hours to spare. Taxis wait outside the terminal; tell them Pebbles Beach by the Garrison. We meet you on the sand with the boards.

Why the Garrison and not the south coast

Every other surf school in Barbados is around Oistins, forty minutes each way from the port on a cruise-day road. We are the only surf school at the Garrison, the closest surf to Bridgetown. More time surfing, less time in a taxi, no watching the clock.

After your lesson

Rinse off at the shop, grab a locally made rash vest or bikini to take home, and you're five minutes from the Garrison Savannah, Carlisle Bay and the boardwalk cafés. Still back in good time.

Questions

Things people ask

What if the ship is late or the surf is flat?

Message us and we move you. No deposit means nothing to lose.

Can we book through the ship?

Not yet. Book direct with us on WhatsApp, it's cheaper and quicker.

Is there somewhere to leave our bags?

At the shop, five minutes' walk, or on the beach with the group.
