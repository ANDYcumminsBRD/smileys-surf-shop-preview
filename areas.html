Where we teach

Surf lessons near you in Barbados

We teach at the Garrison, Freights Bay and wherever the wave is best that day, and we're the closest surf school to Bridgetown, the cruise port and the Hastings hotels. Here's how to reach us from where you're staying.

Surf lessons near Bridgetown & the cruise port

Ten minutes by taxi from the cruise terminal and the city centre. Say 'Pebbles Beach by the Garrison'. Our lessons run on that sand, and the shop is directly above it.

Surf lessons near Hastings & Rockley

Walk or take a two-minute bus along the Hastings road. Guests at Coconut Court, Sugar Bay, Courtyard by Marriott and the Hilton at Needham's Point are closer to our beach than to any other surf school on the island.

Surf lessons near Worthing & St Lawrence Gap

Ten to fifteen minutes by the coast road. We can meet you at the shop or at Freights Bay, whichever is working, and tell you which bus to catch.

Surf lessons near Christ Church & Oistins

Freights Bay is on your doorstep. If your villa is on the south coast we usually meet you there.

Surf lessons near Carlisle Bay & Bay Street

You can walk to us along the boardwalk. Paddleboards, kayaks and the wreck tour all launch from this bay.

Surf lessons near St James & the west coast

Thirty to forty minutes down the ABC Highway. Message us the day before and we'll fix a time that misses the traffic, or ask about board delivery.

Surf lessons near St Michael & the Garrison

Our parish. Brigade House is at the Garrison itself, a UNESCO World Heritage site, with parking nearby and the beach below.
