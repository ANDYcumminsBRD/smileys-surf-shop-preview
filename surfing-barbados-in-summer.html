Surfing in Barbados in summer

The island calls May to October the off-season. For a beginner it might be the best season there is.

The waves

Smaller and gentler than winter, which is exactly what you want for a first lesson. Freights Bay still works most days; the Garrison breaks are at their friendliest.

The water and the weather

Sea at 28 to 29°C. Hotter, a bit more humid, short sharp showers that clear in minutes. Mornings are glorious.

Crowds and prices

September is the quietest month on the island. Empty line-ups, easy bookings, and flights and villas at their lowest.

Hurricane season, honestly

It runs June to November across the Caribbean, but Barbados sits far enough south and east that direct hits are rare. Keep an eye on the forecast like anyone would, and otherwise carry on surfing.

Locals' season

This is when Bajans surf. Kids' holiday camps, after-school sessions, boards being fixed for the winter. Come and join in.
