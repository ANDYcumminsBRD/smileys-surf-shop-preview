Private surf lessons in Barbados

Your own instructor, your own pace. For beginners who want it quieter, and for surfers who want to get better, fast.

Nervous? Private is the quiet way in

Some people don't want an audience for their first go. A private lesson is just you, Trigger and the sea. Nobody waiting their turn, nobody watching, and the lesson goes exactly as fast as you do.

Already surf? This is coaching

If you can stand up but you're stuck, two hours one-to-one with an ISA Level 2 instructor who has surfed Barbados his whole life will move you further than a season of guessing. Pop-up, positioning, reading the wave, turning. We'll film a few waves and show you what we mean.

Surf guiding

Want to surf the island's better waves with someone who knows them, where to sit, what the tide does, who to nod to? Ask about a guided day.

Things people ask

How much is a private lesson?

Message us for the price. It depends on how many of you there are and what you want from it.

Can two of us share a private?

Yes. Two-to-one works well for couples and parent-and-teen.
