Learning to surf at 40, 50 or 60

A large share of our beginners are over forty. Many are over sixty. Nearly all of them stand up. Here's what we've learned teaching them.

Why older beginners do well

You listen. You don't rush the pop-up. You pick waves instead of flailing at all of them. Technique beats energy, and technique is what we teach.

What to tell us

Knees, shoulders, backs, anything that twinges. We change the pop-up to suit, use a bigger board and pick a slower wave. Nobody has to do a press-up.

The water helps

Warm water relaxes muscles and a big soft board floats you. Falling off into 27°C sea isn't falling, it's swimming.

Private or group?

Either. Some prefer the quiet of a private lesson; others love the group laughing with them. Couples often take a two-to-one.

Start here

A two-hour beginner lesson at Pebbles Beach or Freights Bay. Then hire a board for the week and keep going.
