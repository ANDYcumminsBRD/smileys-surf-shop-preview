Who it's for

Complete beginners, nervous swimmers, people who tried once in Cornwall and froze, families, couples and groups. If you can swim, you can do this. Tell us honestly how confident you are in the water and we set the lesson up around it.

How a lesson goes

We meet at the break that's working best that day, usually Freights Bay, Drill Hall or Pebbles Beach. Fifteen minutes on the sand: safety, how a wave works, how to pop up. Then in. Your instructor stays in the water with you, picks your waves and pushes you in until you're catching them yourself. The board is yours till half three.

Why people stand up with us

Small groups mean you get told what you're doing right and wrong straight away. The right board (a big soft-top) and the right wave (waist-deep, slow, sandy) do the rest. It's not a trick. It's just how it should be taught.

Things people ask

Do I need to be able to surf?

No. Most people who come to us have never stood on a board.

Will I stand up in my first lesson?

Almost everyone does. Small groups, the right board, the right wave and an instructor beside you.

What if the surf isn't right?

We move your lesson to another day at no charge. We'd rather you had a great lesson than a flat one.

How do I pay?

Cash on the day, US or Barbados dollars. No deposit, no card.
