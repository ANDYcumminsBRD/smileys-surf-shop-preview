Kayak tour over the Carlisle Bay shipwrecks

Six wrecks in clear, shallow water a few hundred yards off the beach. Paddle out, look down, and watch the fish, turtles and rays go about their day.

Ask about the kayak tour

What you'll see

Carlisle Bay Marine Park holds half a dozen wrecks in water so clear you can see the hulls from your kayak, with turtles, rays and shoals of fish around them. It's the island's most popular snorkelling spot, and most people only ever see it from a crowded catamaran. We take you across it quietly, at your own pace.

Who it's for

Anyone who can sit in a kayak. Doubles for parents and children, singles for the independent. No experience needed, and the water is flat.
